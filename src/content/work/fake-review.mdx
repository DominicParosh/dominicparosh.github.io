## The question

Fake reviews increasingly pair plausible text with stock or recycled images. Does looking at both catch what text alone misses?

## Approach

Transformer text representations (RoBERTa) and visual features (CLIP) are combined with **cross-modal attention fusion**, then classified. Real and synthetic review datasets were prepared for training and testing.
