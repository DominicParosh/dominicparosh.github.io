## The problem

When an order fails, engineers have to search logs across systems to find out where and why, then decide on a fix. <Todo>Volume of failures and how long manual investigation took.</Todo>

## Approach

Elasticsearch log retrieval feeds a set of agents, each with one job:

- **Monitoring** spots the failure.
- **Analysis** traces it through the logs to a likely cause.
- **Remediation** recommends a corrective action, kept *controlled*: it proposes, people approve.
- **Postmortem** writes up what happened.
