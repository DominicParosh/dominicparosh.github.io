## The question

Extraction models produce a relation for every input, even when the evidence isn't there. Can a system learn when *not* to answer, and be honest about its confidence when it does?

## Approach

Three stages: **generator → verifier → calibrated abstention.** The generator proposes candidate relations, the verifier scores whether the text supports them, and Platt scaling turns verifier scores into calibrated probabilities. Below a threshold, the system abstains.
