## The question

Teams argue about RAG vs. agents vs. MCP vs. A2A as if they were competitors. They solve different problems. This study holds the task fixed and changes only the pattern.

## Approach

Four implementations: retrieval-augmented generation; a plan–act–observe–reflect agent; tools exposed over MCP; and cooperating agents over A2A.
