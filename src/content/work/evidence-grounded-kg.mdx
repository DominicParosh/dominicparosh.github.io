## The question

Extraction models produce a relation for every input, even when the text doesn't support it. Once those relations land in a knowledge graph, nobody can tell which edges are real. Can a system attach evidence to every relation it extracts, and decline when it has none?

## Approach

Three stages: **generate → verify → abstain.** A generator proposes entities and relations; a verifier checks each against the source text; Platt scaling turns verifier scores into calibrated probabilities, and below a threshold the relation is dropped instead of guessed.

Every kept relation stores its **provenance**, the source passage that supports it, so the graph in Neo4j stays auditable.
